# Asset credits

The interface uses the bundled Commissioner typeface. Its files are distributed with the SIL Open Font License in [OFL-Commissioner.txt](../dist/assets/OFL-Commissioner.txt).

Earlier local drafts used Satoshi from Fontshare. Its official download's ITF Free Font License restricts redistribution through repositories. Those font files remain in the private local research archive and are excluded from the published source. The public interface works offline with Commissioner; it does not fetch a font from a CDN.

The eleven example atlases are generated variations made from distinct reference photographs. [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md) links the Pinterest sources; [example-provenance.json](example-provenance.json) records available credits, prompts, input/output hashes and inspection limits. Source photographs and downloaded videos are not bundled. Several original photographers could not be independently verified. The project does not grant rights to those source works or claim that the reference-derived examples are cleared for commercial reuse. Their numeric lens settings illustrate an intention rather than measured optical simulation.
